# Recording the demo

Two short screen recordings, no voice and no face: the moments where money moves. Everything
else in the films is recorded from the live site and edited around them, with captions in the
product's own words. Never speed up or cut the moment a stub prints; if it waits for a price,
keep that too — the program refusing to guess is the product working.

Rewritten on 9 October 2026 for the one-card start and Plans. The earlier version (turning the
rule on from `/app/rule`, an S&P 500 arrival) described the product of 24 September.

---

## Before you start (10 minutes)

1. **A weekday, while the Nasdaq 100 has a price** (Take 2 only; Take 1 works any day). Open
   `scrip.work`: on the start card, the Nasdaq 100 chip carries no "waits for a price" note.
   Pre-market prices start around 13:00 Lahore time; the US session runs 18:30–01:00.
2. **Hide balances** in Phantom on the phone (tap your total balance) and in the extension.
3. **The browser.** Chrome, one window, zoom 125%, bookmarks bar hidden, other tabs closed,
   Do Not Disturb on.
4. **Recording.** Laptop: ⌘⇧5 → record the browser window. Phone: its own screen recording
   (iPhone: Control Center → Screen Recording).

---

## Take 1 — "Three taps": starting (about 30 seconds, phone, any day)

This is also the first real test of the start card, so tell me what each Phantom screen said.

You need a **Phantom account that has never touched Scrip**, holding **at least $6 of USDC and
0.03 SOL**. Afterwards, send me its address: it is a team wallet, and the ledger labels it so.

1. On the phone, open Phantom → the browser tab → `scrip.work`.
2. Start the screen recording.
3. Leave everything as it is: **10%**, **Nasdaq 100**, **a first save of $5**.
4. Tap **Start saving 10%**. Approve the sign-in, then approve the transaction.
5. Wait for "You save 10% of every payment" and the line under it with your first save.
6. Tap the first save to open its receipt. Scroll slowly to the end. Stop.

## Take 2 — "Twelve seconds" and the match (about 60 seconds, laptop and phone, weekday)

One payment films both: the automatic save, and ten seconds later your Plan's match on the
same receipt. It uses the **Demo** account (`6mCB…fKDE`), which saves 20% of every payment into
the Nasdaq 100 and is a member of your Plan.

1. On the laptop, sign in at `scrip.work/app` with the **Demo** account. Start recording the
   window.
2. On the phone, start the screen recording.
3. On the phone, in Phantom (your main account): Send → USDC → paste the Demo address →
   **$20** → send.
4. On the laptop, touch nothing. Wait until the stub prints, then five seconds more.
5. Click the new stub to open its receipt. Wait there: within about twenty seconds the green
   row **"Added by @shariq"** appears by itself. Hold it for five seconds. Stop both recordings.

This uses $2 of the Demo account's $5 monthly match and about $2 of the Plan's escrow. Top the
Plan up afterwards (Take 3).

## Take 3 — the Plan, from the sponsor's side (about 40 seconds, laptop, any day)

1. Sign in at `scrip.work/app/org/plans` with your main account and start recording.
2. Scroll slowly over the Plan: its terms, what is in the escrow, the member, matched so far.
3. Under **Add to the escrow**, type the amount you are adding and press the button; approve.
4. Wait for the escrow figure to change. Stop.

## Then

Put the files in `~/Movies/scrip-demo/` (AirDrop the phone's recordings to the Mac) and name
them `take1-phone`, `take2-laptop`, `take2-phone`, `take3-laptop`.

From those recordings and the live site, the edit makes:

- **the judges' film** — about two minutes, captions instead of voice: starting, a payment
  saving itself, the match, the ledger, what a stranger can verify;
- **clips for posts** — "Three taps", "Twelve seconds", "Whoever pays you can match it".

What the films never claim: returns, projections, user counts that are not on the ledger,
anything about a token, and nothing about how Scrip's servers work. If a sentence cannot be
checked by opening a page in the film, it does not go in.
